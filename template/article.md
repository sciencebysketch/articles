### Embedding a Slideshow:
To embed an interactive slideshow inside your article:
1. Create a subfolder inside your article directory (e.g. `my-slideshow/`).
2. Add your slides (`1.png`, `2.png`, etc.) and an `information.yml`.
3. In this markdown file, embed it anywhere using:
::slideshow[my-slideshow]
